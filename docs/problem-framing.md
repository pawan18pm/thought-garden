# Problem Framing — Thought Garden (working title)

> Version: V0.1 · Date: 2026-11-19 · Type: Personal PM portfolio project
> Platform: iOS app (React Native + Expo)

## Problem Statement

Students and early-career learners juggling studies, interview prep, and side
projects have far more thoughts than time: things to learn, tasks to finish, and
ideas to build. These thoughts show up at random moments, mid-lecture or
halfway through a YouTube tutorial. They get lost, or they end up in notes apps
with no order, so the important ones get buried and progress feels invisible.
To-do apps don't fix this. They assume you've already decided what to do, and
missing a day makes people feel guilty.

Thought Garden gives these learners a calm, instant place to **plant** a
thought the moment it appears, sort it into their own topic **beds**, and
**water** it each time they work on it until it **blooms** (done). A
calendar-style **Garden** shows what they planted, watered, and finished each
day, so progress becomes visible and rewarding without guilt. For the business,
the goal is to prove a daily return habit (Day-7 retention of 30%+), the
foundation for a future freemium "Pro" tier. As a portfolio piece, it shows
end-to-end product thinking from problem to shipped app.

---

| Field | Description |
|-------|-------------|
| **WHO** | **Primary:** self-driven college students and job-seekers (about 17 to 25) who learn from many sources (lectures, YouTube, courses) and are pursuing several goals at once, for example interview prep, design skills, AI fundamentals, side projects. **Secondary:** students who feel overwhelmed and need gentle structure rather than pressure. **Why this group:** they have the most thoughts per day competing for limited time, they capture on the go (phone, during study breaks), and they're underserved by tools built for working professionals. |
| **WHAT Problem** | 1. **Thoughts get lost.** Ideas come up mid-study and vanish before they're written down. 2. **No order.** Captured thoughts sit in notes apps, chats, or screenshots with no topic or priority. 3. **Invisible progress.** Partial effort (studying a topic across three sessions) isn't recorded anywhere, so students feel they did nothing. 4. **Guilt-driven tools.** To-do and streak apps punish missed days, and students quit them. **Current workarounds:** notes app, WhatsApp "message to self", paper sticky notes, morning lists that never get reviewed. |
| **WHEN** | **During study:** a thought appears while watching a lecture or reading, and needs capturing in about 3 seconds without breaking focus. **Morning:** deciding what matters today. **Study breaks or evening:** logging what was worked on or finished. **Weekly:** looking back at progress. |
| **WHAT Job** | "When a thought comes to me while I'm studying, help me catch it instantly, put it where it belongs, and see it grow as I work on it, so nothing important gets lost and I can see I'm making progress." |
| **WHAT benefits for the customer** | Capture in under 3 seconds with nothing to decide. Thoughts organized into **their own** topic beds (e.g. Interview, Design, Claude Code, AI Fundamentals). Effort is visible even before something is finished (watering). A calm, playful, guilt-free experience: no punishment for missed days. A clear daily and weekly picture of what they've grown. |
| **WHAT benefits for the company** | **MVP (validation):** prove the habit with 10 to 20 student testers. Targets: 60%+ plant a thought on day 1 (activation), 30%+ active on Day 7, 15%+ active on Day 30. **Later (revenue):** a freemium model where the core loop stays free forever and "Pro" (unlimited beds, sync, insights, themes, widgets) converts a target of 2 to 5% of active users. **Portfolio:** a documented end-to-end case study (problem, decisions, deliberate cuts, metrics, shipped app). |
